The Smart Engine

How StockWise Works in 3 Simple Steps

Never lose track of expiry dates or guess what needs reordering.

1

Delivery Intake with Batches

When 100 cartons of milk arrive, StockWise records a separate batch with its specific expiry date and purchase cost. Deliveries of the same product never get mixed up.

2

First Expired, First Out (FEFO)

When a cashier records a customer sale, the system automatically draws from the oldest eligible non-expired batch first. Expired stock is automatically locked from sale.

3

Automated Alerts & Margins

Managers get instant warnings when milk is 3 days from expiry or bread is low in stock. The owner reviews true gross profit after batch purchase costs (COGS).
